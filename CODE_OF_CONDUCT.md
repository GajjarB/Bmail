# Code of conduct

Bmail is built for people having a hard time: job hunting, waiting on answers,
keeping on top of their mail. Treat everyone here the same way you would want to
be treated on a bad week.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- No harassment, insults, or comments about anyone's background, identity,
  language or level of experience.
- Never post someone else's private information, and that includes their mail.

The maintainer may edit or remove anything that breaks this, and may block
anyone who keeps breaking it. To report something privately, use the form in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); it reaches only the maintainer.
